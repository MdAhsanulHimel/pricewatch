Price Watch settings

Checks run silently in the background while Edge is open. Nothing opens a window or takes focus.

Schedule

This is the default. Any item can have its own interval (open its menu in the popup). A page that fails three times in a row is checked less and less often, up to once a day, until it works again; after six failures you get one alert asking you to re-pick it. If the browser was closed for longer than one interval, a catch-up check runs a few minutes after you next start it.

Alerts

Which changes are worth an alert

Target alerts (set per item) always fire. Ignored changes are still recorded in the history and the price database.

Quiet hours

to

Alerts that happen during quiet hours are sent as one summary when they end. Applies to Windows, Telegram and webhook alerts.

Phone and chat alerts

Telegram

  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and copy the token it gives you.
  2. Open your new bot and send it any message, for example hi.
  3. Paste the token below and press Find my chat ID.

Webhook

Sends JSON with text (Slack), content (Discord) and an events list with product, url, old and new price, currency and stock. Works with Discord, Slack, ntfy, Home Assistant, n8n, Zapier and similar. The token and webhook address stay on this PC and are left out of backups.

Hard-to-read sites

Off by default. Pages are normally read by a plain download, or rendered in a hidden frame inside the extension, which never shows on screen. A few sites refuse both. With this on, those sites are opened in an inactive, muted tab for a few seconds, only when you have been away from the keyboard for two minutes, or when you press Check now yourself. It never opens a new window.

Price database

A row is added only when something happens: the first reading of an item, a price change, or a switch between in stock and out of stock. Columns: Product name, Product Link, Date, Price, Stock. Opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

Backup

The backup holds your items, their history, the price database and your settings (without the Telegram token and webhook address). Import replaces your current list. Site permissions are not part of the backup, so after importing on a new PC, open the popup and press Re-authorise on each item.
